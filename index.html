Rules of the Road Regulations (SI 76 of 2022)
  • Pedestrians: no crossing a public road with head or ear phones or while on a mobile phone. Fine up to K300 (1,000 penalty units).
  • Pedestrians wait for the lights to turn red for vehicles. Drivers give way to a pedestrian on a crossing not controlled by a sign, warden, inspector or officer.
  • Buses stop only at a bus stop or bus station. No vehicle stops at a bus stop longer than 3 minutes. Fine up to K300 or 3 months in prison, or both.
  • Heavy goods vehicles keep 50 m behind a heavy goods vehicle in free-flowing traffic.
  • Keep 50 m behind an emergency vehicle. Fine up to K300 or up to 3 years in prison, or both.
PSV and fleet rules (RTSA Fleet Safety Management Guide, 2018)
  • Drive no more than 4 hours, then take a 30 minute break. 15 minutes every 2 hours is recommended.
  • Sleep at least 7 hours before the next shift. Keep driving and rest records for 12 months.
  • Both hands on the wheel. No phone, radio or electronic device while driving. Seat belts always.
  • No alcohol, illegal drugs or impairing medication.
  • Pre-trip check: 360 degree walk-around, windscreen, fuel, plates, tyres, spare wheel and jack, secure load, fire extinguisher (9 kg commercial, 2.5 kg small vehicles).
  • Journeys of 4 hours or more need approved rest areas and emergency support in the plan.
  • PSV fleets must carry GPS tracking (SI 78 of 2016). Night driving needs a fleet safety policy and exemption.
  • Crash facts: about 2,000 deaths a year (2014 to 2017). 61% were pedestrians, cyclists and motorcyclists. Human error caused over 87%.
Ten basic rules of driving (RTSA, Daily Nation, 3 Sept 2021)
  • Know the Highway Code. Concentrate fully. Read the traffic situation before you act.
  • Be patient and hang back when in doubt. Drive with care and courtesy.
  • Adapt your speed to conditions and never exceed limits.
  • Develop car sense. Use the horn only as a warning and give proper signals.
  • Keep the vehicle roadworthy. Perfect your roadcraft and acknowledge courtesies.

Rules to remember

Alcohol (111): no more than 80 mg/100 ml. PSV drivers (113): no intoxicating liquor or narcotics when driving.

Gap (141, 181): at least 2 seconds on fast roads, doubled when wet. About 1 m per km/h on open tarred roads.

Gravel (185): 60 km/h outside residential areas, 40 km/h in residential areas.

White lines (143, 144): unbroken centre lines are hazard warning lines. If the line nearest you is broken you may overtake with caution.

Roundabouts: give way to traffic on your right unless markings say otherwise. Keep left unless overtaking or turning right.

Phones: no hand-held phone while driving. Motorcyclists: wear an appropriate helmet.

Sources: Zambian Highway Code 6th ed.; SI 50 of 2020; SI 76 of 2022; SI 90 of 2016; RTSA Fleet Safety Management Guide (March 2018).

Examiner Tricks

"Drive through that red light, nobody is coming."
Refuse politely. Crossing a red light is illegal.

"Overtake over the double solid line."
Refuse. Crossing continuous lines is forbidden.

"Skip the pre-trip check to save time."
Refuse. The check is mandatory.

Yard Manoeuvres

Hill start. Handbrake on, find the clutch bite point, add gas, release handbrake smoothly. Rolling back fails the test.

Parallel parking. Check all mirrors constantly. Never touch the kerb.

Three-point turn. Blind-spot check before every gear change. Turn lock to lock smoothly.
